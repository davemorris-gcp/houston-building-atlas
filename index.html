About The Houston Building Atlas

The Houston Building Atlas is an interactive historical cartography project created by Preservation Houston to help residents, historians, architects, and urban planners explore how Houston's neighborhoods took shape from 1836 to the present.

Where Does the Data Come From?

  • Harris Central Appraisal District (HCAD): Tax parcel boundaries, earliest structure completion year (Yr_Impr / date_erect), remodel year, total building square footage, land area, and land use classification.
  • City of Houston Planning & Development (Historic Preservation Office): Authoritative Historic District parcel inventories (Contributing vs. Non-Contributing status), Designated Landmarks (LM), Protected Landmarks (PLM), Heritage Districts, National Register Districts, and Decade-by-Decade Annexation Boundaries.
  • OpenStreetMap & Architectural Footprint Synthesis: Observed building footprint polygons spatially joined to HCAD tax parcels, supplemented by oriented architectural footprints derived from parcel geometry and CAMA building floor area.
  • Texas Historical Commission (THC): Official state historical markers across Harris County.

Things to Keep in Mind

  • Oldest Structure Rule: When more than one structure exists on a property, the property is dated and color-coded based on the oldest recorded structure on the site, cross-referenced against City of Houston landmark records.
  • Appraisal Date Limitations: HCAD completion dates are compiled for tax appraisal purposes and may be approximate for 19th- and early 20th-century structures.
  • Untaxed & Exempt Properties: Vacant parcels and certain tax-exempt civic properties (schools, churches, parks, government buildings) may lack completion dates in HCAD and appear in warm pewter gray unless documented in City of Houston landmark records.
