Why the Current Detail Sidebar Feels Crowded & How We Can Fix It Without Losing Any Functionality

Below is the exact same historic property (1435 Heights Blvd, Miller-Vance House, Built 1914) rendered in the Current Sidebar ("Before") alongside 4 Best-Practice UX Redesign Options modeled on Apple/Google Maps, Zillow/Redfin, Linear/Notion's Simple-vs-Pro toggle, and Wikipedia/NYT Scrollytelling. Every option preserves 100% of your existing tools while dramatically reducing visual clutter.

🔍 Audit of Current Sidebar Crowding (380px width):
  • 9 Stacked Header Bars Above Tabs: Badges, title, name provenance, AKA chips, subtitle, status bar, and alert banners push tabs & photos down.
  • Box-in-Box Overload: 4-box Protection Scorecard + giant Ch. 42 CTA + 12 bordered fact cells = 18 nested boxes in Tab 1 alone.
  • 35+ Competing Buttons/Chips: Every fact cell repeats 🔎, Outline, 🎯 Isolate, and ⚑ Flag, followed by a 6-button vertical stack at the bottom.
