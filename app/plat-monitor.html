The Houston Building Atlas automatically fetches and enriches recent biweekly Planning Commission Plat Spreadsheets, Section K Development Plat Variances (DPV), and Public Hearing Notices. You can also drop any Plat Tracker spreadsheet (.xlsx or .csv) or saved work file (.json) below.

.xlsx, .csv, or .json

  1. The tool sorts every item into To check, Designated, ETJ, and Screened out, following Preservation Houston's Plat Report Monitoring Guide, and adds Section K Development Plat Variances (DPV) and Section III/IV Public Hearing notices from the Agenda Outline PDF.
  2. Every item is pre-cross-referenced against 1,512,020 Harris County building footprints, 1,546,683 tax parcels, 5,350 historic platted subdivisions, City Landmarks, Historic Districts, Good Brick Awards, and active COH/DSHS/TDLR permits.
  3. Switch to the Property Watchlist tab at any time to track endangered properties across all 15 municipal, county, and state checkpoints, with automated daily 7:00 AM CT sweeps.
