## 4. External Third-Party Server Integrations, Dependencies & Failure-Resilience Matrix

Because the Houston Building Atlas is hosted at **$0/month on GitHub Pages** with **zero proprietary backend servers to maintain**, it is critical to distinguish **which features are 100% locally bundled in Git** (immune to third-party server downtime) versus **which features connect to external third-party servers at browser runtime, during scheduled GitHub Actions runs, or as outbound user research links**.

### 4.1 Tier A: Locally Bundled Static Assets on GitHub Pages (Zero Third-Party Runtime Dependency)
If every municipal, county, state, and university server in Texas went offline simultaneously, **100% of the core Atlas continues to load and operate** from `app/public/` on GitHub's global Fastly CDN:
- All `1,507,897` dated building footprints (`houston_buildings_{nw_w,nw_e,ne,sw,se}.pmtiles` + `buildings.geojson`) and `2,406` `curated_overrides.json` entries.
- All `23` vector overlays (`overlays.json`, including `622` Lost Houston structures), `4,242` precomputed Neighborhood & Council District Preservation Scorecards (`neighborhood_scorecards.json`), `25,892` instant search entries (`search_index.json`), `90` gzipped countywide street-address shards (`address_shards/addr_10..99.json.gz`, `14.52 MiB`), `152` gzipped HCAD permit & stewardship shards (`permits/permits_001..970.json.gz`, `73.41 MiB`), `152` gzipped 22-year (`2005-2026`) HCAD ownership chain shards (`owner_history/owners_001..970.json.gz`, `47.72 MiB`), `5,576` HAIF thread mappings (`haif_index.json`), `6,169` COH Historic District parcel survey rows (`hpo_district_surveys.json`, `1.04 MiB`), `595` TARO 2.0 & HPL architectural records (`archival_index.json`), `citydir_index.json` (incl. `1927-1963` USCO public-domain audit metadata), `historical_audit_summary.json`, `hahc_coa_tracker.json`, `active_permit_alerts.json`, `plat_agenda_monitor.json`, `property_watchlist.json` (`27` confirmed dossiers), and `monitor_activity_log.json` (`90-day` Central Time daily run ledger & event history), totaling **`625.02 MiB`** across `app/` (`354.98 MiB` below the `980 MiB` hard ceiling).
- All **`504` UT Austin PCL Sanborn WebP plates** (`app/public/data/sanborn_ut/` - completely immune to UT's Anubis PoW gate) and all **`65` mirrored Subdivision Deed Restriction PDFs** (`app/public/deeds/`).
- `Solid Dark` (`#0B0E11`) and `Solid Light` (`#F5F2EB`) basemap modes and the client-side **Vector `.SVG` / `.PNG` / `.GeoJSON` Export Studio** require zero external tile servers!

## 5. Manual Curation Playbook for the General Public (No Login Required)

Anyone - homeowners, neighborhood historians, architects, students, or field volunteers - can contribute archival research and field observations directly from the live map without creating an account. **Public submissions never overwrite live public map data directly**; every submission is saved immediately in the contributor's own browser (`localStorage` preview) and appended as `status = "Pending"` in Preservation Houston's Moderation Google Sheet (`Pending_Submissions` tab).

### 5.1 Submitting a Verified Construction Year, Architect, Builder, or Style Correction
1. Click any building footprint on the map to open the **Property Inspector**, then click **`✎ Suggest Correction`** (`#btn-open-correction`).
2. Use the **History** tab's **5-Step House History Research Kit** (Sanborn Fire Insurance sheets `1877-1955` and HPL City Directories `1866-1926`) to verify when the house first appeared.
3. Enter the **Corrected Year Built** (`1836-2026`), optional **Architect / Architectural Style**, **Builder / General Contractor**, and **Original Permit # / Contract Cost**.
4. Select the **Primary Historical / Verification Source** (e.g., *Houston City Directory*, *Sanborn Fire Insurance Map*, *HMRC Contractor Portfolio / TGCA Bulletin*, *Historic Deed / Mechanic's Lien*) and paste the specific volume/page citation or archive URL.
5. Click **`Submit Correction for Admin Review`**. Your browser immediately previews your suggested date with a `"Pending Your Local Review"` badge while queuing the row for Preservation Houston staff review.

### 5.2 Contributing Historical Building Names & Colloquial `AKA` Aliases
- In `#correction-modal`, fill in **`Building Name & Historical / Colloquial Aliases`**, separating multiple historical tenant or colloquial names with `/` or `;` (for example: `Cheek-Neal Coffee Building / Sampson Street Lofts / Maxwell House Plant`).
- Upon staff approval, the primary name displays at the top of the Property Inspector, secondary names appear as `AKA` badges, and **every alias becomes searchable** in the global autocomplete bar.

### 5.3 Contributing Historical or Present-Day Photographs & Subdivision Deed Restriction PDFs
- **Photographs:** Expand **`📷 Contribute a Historic or Present-Day Photograph`** in `#correction-modal` and paste a direct public image URL (Wikimedia Commons, HPL Digital Archives, Portal to Texas History, etc.), photograph year/era (`c. 1935`), and archive credit. (If an automated Wikimedia image on a parcel is off-topic, any user can also click **`✕ Hide Photo`** locally and submit a better archival photo URL.)
- **Subdivision Deed Restrictions:** Expand **`📜 Contribute a Subdivision Deed Restriction PDF or Clerk File #`** and share a civic association deed PDF link or Harris County Clerk File / Film Code number (e.g., `Clerk File #T699546`) so staff can mirror and index it into `app/public/deeds/`.

### 5.4 Flagging Building Footprint Shape, Rotation, Demolished/Vacant Lots, or Missing-Structure Errors
1. Click **`✎ Suggest Correction`** (or **`📐 Report Missing or Wrong Footprint`**), then click **`Inspect Roof in Satellite View ↗`** to view the parcel at Zoom 20 on aerial imagery.
2. Under **`📐 Building Footprint Shape or Orientation Issue`**, select the exact geometry defect:
   - *Wrong building shape / Generic rectangular box*
   - *Wrong orientation / Rotated angle*
   - *Missing main building (only garage / outbuilding shown)*
   - *Merged with adjacent building(s)*
   - *Overlapping / conflicting polygons*
   - *Shifted / offset from satellite roof*
   - *Building demolished - lot currently vacant (hide footprint)* (auto-fills `0` in **Corrected Year Built**; free-text notes are optional - upon staff approval or **Staff Admin -> Publish Live**, the ghost building footprint is hidden immediately via `suppress_only: true` and the parcel renders as a vacant `year_built: 0` lot in Parcels view)
   - *Building demolished & replaced by newer structure* (enter the replacement building's `1836-2026` construction year in **Corrected Year Built** and briefly note the replacement structure)
3. Add a 1-sentence description when reporting shape/orientation or replacement defects (e.g., *"Only the rear garage apartment is outlined; the 1924 L-shaped bungalow fronting Key St is missing"*) and submit. (You can also type `0` directly into **Corrected Year Built** as a standard numeric code to mark a demolished building on a currently vacant lot.) The row is automatically tagged **`📐 Footprint / Geometry Issue`** in the admin workbook.

### 5.5 Reporting Physical Site Demolition Signals (`[SITE ACTIVITY OBSERVATION]`)
- If you walk or drive past an older building and spot **wooden TABC 60-day liquor signs, blue/white replat or variance signs, commercial broker/leasing banners, temporary chain-link demolition fencing, roll-off dumpsters, asbestos warning tape, or utility disconnect paint markings** (which appear days or weeks before digital permits post online):
  1. Open the building in the Houston Building Atlas on your phone or desktop and click **`🚨 Report Site Activity to PH`** (available on Active Permit Alert banners and inside the Inspector).
  2. Describe what is posted or happening on site (and optionally paste a photo link) and submit.
  3. The submission enters `Pending_Submissions` prefixed with **`[SITE ACTIVITY OBSERVATION]`** so Preservation Houston advocacy staff can inspect and escalate to the 15-Point Endangered Property Watchlist.

### 5.6 One-Click Flagging of Misattributed Photos, Unverified Automated Claims & Narrative Errors
- **`⚑ Wrong Building` (Parcel Photographs):** If an automated Wikimedia Commons, Geograph, or forum photo depicts an adjacent building across the property line, a demolished predecessor, or a non-building subject, click **`⚑ Wrong Building`** below the photo. The image disappears immediately in your browser and posts a structured `PHOTO_MISATTRIBUTION_FLAG` ticket (`hcad_num`, `photo_url`) to `Pending_Submissions`. Once approved by Preservation Houston staff, that photo URL is suppressed globally for all visitors on that parcel.
- **`⚑ Flag` / `⚑ Flag Name` (Tier 3/4 Automated Claims):** Unverified HAIF forum names, rounded HCAD decade estimates (`c. 1920 (HCAD Est.)`), and automated enrichments display their evidentiary tier (`Tier 1` through `Tier 4`) alongside a 1-click **`⚑ Flag`** button (`CLAIM_DISPUTE_FLAG`). Clicking it hides the disputed unverified name/claim locally right away and queues it for moderator review (and global suppression upon approval).
- **`⚑ Flag Error` (Editorial Narratives & Primary-Source Citations):** Every Guided Chapter (`6`), Portfolio Monograph (`27`), Curated Walking Tour (`9`), Deed Restriction Digest (`30`), and *Lost Houston* / historical overlay card includes clickable primary-source `.narrative-citation-pill` links and a 1-click **`⚑ Flag Error`** button (`NARRATIVE_ERROR_FLAG`) so historians and readers can report any conflation or typo in 5 seconds.
