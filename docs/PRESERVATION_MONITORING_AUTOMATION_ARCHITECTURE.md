## 1. Overview of the Three Preservation Monitoring Workspaces

Preservation Houston operates three integrated workspaces inside `app/plat-monitor.html` designed with Emily Ardoin:

1. **Workspace Mode 1: Biweekly Planning Commission Plat & Variance Review (`mode="agenda"`)**
   - Screens 100 to 140 subdivision plats, replats, Section I.K Development Plat Variances (`DPV`), and Section III/IV Public Hearing notices every two weeks ahead of the Thursday 2:30 p.m. Houston Planning Commission meeting across rolling multi-cycle history (`October 29, 2026`, `October 15, 2026`, `October 1, 2026`, and `All Recent Cycles`).
   - Distinguishes posted Thursday meeting agenda items (`Agenda #N`, `onMeetingAgenda === true`; `94` items on `2026-10-29`) from biweekly cycle applications still in staff review or deferred off the posted agenda (`Cycle Filing Only`, `onMeetingAgenda === false`; `44` items on `2026-10-29`) via the **`Agenda Presence`** filter (`#fAgendaPresence`).
2. **Workspace Mode 2: 15-Point Endangered Property Multi-Agency Watchlist & Dossier Workstation (`mode="watchlist"`)**
   - Replaces manual multi-property tracking spreadsheets with a continuous 15-point dossier covering city, county, state, and commercial signals across four stages: `Every Check`, `Sale & Ownership`, `Plans & Use`, and `Demolition Signals`.
   - Contains strictly human-confirmed dossiers (`tierConfirmed === true` or manually added properties; `confirmed_baseline_apps = set()`, zero unconfirmed baseline machine seeds).
   - Uses a unified **Street-Address-First** sidebar convention across modes (**Street Address** as the bold primary title, **Historic Landmark / Project Name** as the subtitle), sorts Watchlist properties strictly `Tier 1 -> Tier 2 -> Tier 3`, provides a 1-click header **Tier switcher** syncing bidirectionally back to all matching Biweekly Monitor cycles (`syncWatchlistTierToPlatReviews`), separates **`Archive (Tier 3)`** (preserving resolved historic dossiers) from **`Remove from Watchlist`** (writing cloud-synced `REMOVED_WATCHLIST_TOMBSTONE` markers for false alarms), and tracks **Broker / Listing Agent** (`broker`) alongside **Tracker Status** (`Active / High Priority`, `Dormant / Medium Priority`, `For Sale`, `Monitoring`, `Saved`, `Lost`, `Archived (Tier 3)`) and **Threat Category** (`Significant Building Threatened`, `Neighborhood / District Context Impact`, `Lot Split / Subdividing Historic Lot`, `Setback / Walkable Realm Variance`, `Institutional / Cultural Site`, `No Historic Impact`).
3. **Workspace Mode 3: What's New & Daily Log Morning Briefing (`mode="activity"`)**
   - Unifies all four preservation early-warning feeds (`Watchlist Sweeps`, `Plat Agendas`, `Staff Edits`, and `Permits & HAHC`) into a single-screen morning briefing paired with a trailing **90-day `America/Chicago` Daily Run Ledger**, per-browser unread watermarking (`✓ Mark Caught Up`), 1-click **`+ Promote to Watchlist`** dossier creation from citywide permit alerts, inline `● New` / `● Updated` indicators across the Agenda and Watchlist tabs, and remembered last-active tab routing (`phpam:lastMode`).

## 3. Workflow B (15-Point Property Watchlist): Daily 8-Agency Sweeps, 3-Way Cloud Reconciliation & Assisted Controls

Out of the 15 monitoring points in the Endangered Property Watchlist, **8 points are swept automatically every day at 7:00 AM Central Time (`12:00 UTC`)**, and **7 points provide policy-compliant assisted launchers** tailored to each agency's search rules.

### Part 3A: The 8 Daily Automated Checklist Points & Ownership-Partitioned 3-Way Reconciliation

Every day at 7:00 AM Central Time (`.github/workflows/update_live_permits.yml`), `fetch_plat_and_watchlist.py` pulls live staff Watchlist edits from `Plat_&_Watchlist_Sync`, executes `sweep_watched_property_agencies()` across every watched property (matching all perimeter street addresses, 13-digit HCAD accounts, and discovered snowball `entities`, `dbas`, `officers`, and `projectNumbers`), and stamps `lastAutoSweepAt` and `[AUTO-SWEEP]` findings by stable `row.id` (`chk-01..chk-15`):

1. **Check #4: Corporate Entity Verification (`TX Comptroller Sales & Franchise Tax`)**
   - **Automated Endpoints:** `data.texas.gov` Socrata REST APIs for Active Sales Tax Permit Holders (`https://data.texas.gov/resource/jrea-zgmq.json`) and Active Franchise Taxpayers (`https://data.texas.gov/resource/9cir-efmm.json`), querying Harris County / Houston perimeter street addresses and known LLC/DBA names (`Smith St C Store LLC`, `OB Texas Properties LLC`).
   - **Assisted Fallbacks (when no franchise tax filing exists yet):** Direct `Portal Resources` links to Texas Comptroller Taxable Entity Search (`https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/franchise/account-status/search`), Bizapedia (`https://www.bizapedia.com/`), Google Web Search, and TX SOSDirect (`https://direct.sos.state.tx.us/acct/acct-login.asp`).
2. **Check #5: HCAD Ownership & Classification (`HCADGIS` / CAMA)**
   - **Automated Endpoints:** Live HCAD ArcGIS REST MapServer (`https://arcweb.hcad.org/server/rest/services/public/public_query/MapServer/0/query`) and annual HCAD CAMA real property tables, detecting owner-of-record transfers, mailing address changes, valuation shifts, and state land-use reclassifications.
3. **Check #6: Liquor Licensing (`TABC Public Inquiry & Open Data`)**
   - **Automated Endpoints:** `data.texas.gov` Socrata REST APIs for TABC Pending Original License Applications (`https://data.texas.gov/resource/mxm5-tdpj.json`, queried on `address` and `owner` in Harris County because `mxm5-tdpj` omits `trade_name`) and Active Licenses (`https://data.texas.gov/resource/7hf9-qc9f.json`, queried across `address`, `owner`, and `trade_name` with brand-variant expansion such as `Brake Time` / `Braketime`).
4. **Check #9: Replats & Variances (`COH Planning Commission Agenda & Notices`)**
   - **Automated Endpoints:** Biweekly COH Plat Spreadsheets (`https://www.houstontx.gov/planning/DevelopRegs/dev_reports.html`), Agenda Outline PDFs (`Section I.K DPV` & Section III/IV Hearings), and Public Hearing Notice PDFs (`https://www.houstontx.gov/planning/notices/`). Because Microsoft PowerApps PlatTracker restricts login to registered applicants/surveyors with no public search interface, Check #9 links directly to the in-app Biweekly Plat Review, `dev_reports.html`, and Planning Commission Notices instead of the login wall.
5. **Check #10: Accessibility Registration (`TDLR Architectural Barriers - TABS`)**
   - **Automated Endpoint & Canonical UI Link:** Posts `application/x-www-form-urlencoded` DataTables queries (`build_tdlr_tabs_search_params`, omitting empty filter keys so server-side filters bind cleanly) to `https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/TABS/Search/SearchProjects` with `LocationCounty="2101"` (Harris County) and enriches matched projects via `https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/TABS/Search/Project/{ProjectNumber}` for commercial construction or renovation registrations >= \$50,000, while linking reviewers directly to `https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/tabs/search`.
6. **Check #13: Code Complaints & Dangerous Buildings (`Houston 311` + `DONDB*.csv` Cross-Checker)**
   - **Automated Endpoint & Periodic City List Cross-Checker:** Automatically sweeps the City of Houston 311 ArcGIS FeatureServer (`HOUSTON311_RECENT_SR_SNOW/FeatureServer/0`) for open structural/nuisance complaints, and pairs with the in-browser **`Import & Cross-Check Dangerous Buildings CSV`** workstation (`parseAndMatchDangerousBuildingsCsv`) for periodic City of Houston Department of Neighborhoods `DONDB*.csv` exports (`CaseNumber`, `Parcel.ParcelID` with 10-to-12-digit zero-padding to 13 digits and scientific-notation warnings, `Parcel.ParcelName`, `Parcel.Section`, `Parcel.Area.Code`, `ProcessState.Code`, as well as street-split CSV layouts).
7. **Check #14: Demolition, Sewer Disconnect & Building Permits (`COH Sold Permits`)**
   - **Automated Endpoints:** City of Houston Sold Permits portal (`https://cohtora.houstontx.gov/approot/soldpermits/online_permit.htm`) and `active_permit_alerts.json` (`fetch_live_permits.py`). Redundant weekly summary `Permit Activity Reports` links are omitted on per-property dossiers because Sold Permits already contains every individual permit record and automatically harvests 8-digit ILMS Project Numbers into `searchTerms.projectNumbers` for Check #8 (`ILMS`).
8. **Check #15: Asbestos & Demolition Notifications (`TX DSHS`)**
   - **Automated Endpoint:** Texas Department of State Health Services Asbestos Notification search (`https://vo.ras.dshs.state.tx.us/datamart/notificationSearchAction.do`), catching mandatory 10-working-day advance abatement and demolition filings.

**Ownership-Partitioned 3-Way Watchlist Reconciliation (`reconcileWatchlistProperty`):**  
Previously, when `Plat_&_Watchlist_Sync` held a cloud sheet row saved on an earlier date (such as October 23, 2026), loading the cloud sheet could overwrite newer automated pipeline findings or seeded snowball `searchTerms` in `property_watchlist.json`. Both `fetch_plat_and_watchlist.py` and `app/js/watchlistSyncHelpers.js` now enforce **field-level ownership partitioning**:
- **Pipeline-Owned Telemetry** (`lastAutoSweepAt` and `[AUTO-SWEEP]` checklist findings deduplicated by `getAutoSweepDedupeKey(rowId, entry)`) is union-merged by `row.id` (`chk-01..chk-15`) so fresh daily sweeps are never wiped by older Cloud Sheet or `localStorage` rows.
- **Human-Owned Research** (`tier`, `tierConfirmed`, `trackerStatus`, `removedFromWatchlist`, `threatCategory`, `legalDescription`, `neighborhood`, `broker`, `designFirm` hydrated seamlessly from legacy `architectEngineer`, `phPastActions`, `reportedBy`, `driveLinks` tracked via `driveLinksUpdatedAt`, `searchTerms` tracked via `searchTermsUpdatedAt`, and per-checkpoint manual `status`, `statusChangedAt`, `lastChecked`, `checks[].loggedAt`, and `checks[].links`) always preserves staff edits and timestamped deletions (`REMOVED_WATCHLIST_TOMBSTONE` / `REMOVED_TIER3_TOMBSTONE`) from Cloud Sync / `localStorage`.

## 4. Workstation Features Supporting Multi-Property Investigations

1. **Snowball Search-Term Discovery Panel (`searchTerms`):**
   - Real-world investigations often chain across agencies: on the 1894 John Henry Kirby Mansion (`2000 Smith St.`), searching `"2000 Smith"` and perimeter `"530 Pierce"` uncovered TABC and TDLR TABS (`TABS2026022839`) filings naming `"Smith St C Store LLC"`, `"Brake Time"` / `"Braketime Smith Road"`, and `"Heights Engineering"`; Bizapedia and Comptroller lookups uncovered `"Omair Bashir"` and `"BTCM National Retail Operations LLC"`; searching Block `"12"` and Subdivision `"Hadley"` on Harris County Clerk `RP.aspx` uncovered two same-day warranty deeds revealing `"OB Texas Properties LLC"`.
   - Adding a discovered term (`entities`, `dbas`, `officers`, `legalKeywords`, `altAddresses`, or `projectNumbers`) stores it cleanly on the dossier for reference during manual portal checks and automatically includes that identifier across all subsequent daily 8-agency automated sweeps (`#4`, `#5`, `#6`, `#9`, `#10`, `#13`, `#14`, `#15`).
2. **Inline Property Metadata Editing, Broker Tracking, Explicit Watchlist Removal & Sanitized Document Links:**
   - Every watched property includes a 1-click header **Tier Switcher** (`Tier 1`, `Tier 2`, `Tier 3`, syncing directly back to matching Biweekly Monitor cycles), separate **`Archive (Tier 3)`** vs. **`Remove from Watchlist`** actions, and an **`Edit Property Details`** form (`Address`, `Secondary Perimeter Addresses`, `Historic / Property Name`, `13-Digit HCAD Account(s)`, `Year Built`, `Legal Description`, `Priority Tier`, `Tracker Status`, `Threat Category`, `Designation / Historic Significance`, `Neighborhood`, `Current Owner`, `Buyer / Developer / Tenant Entities`, `Broker`, `Design / Engineering Firm`, `PH Past Actions`, and `Reported By`).
   - Both the property header (`driveLinks`) and individual checklist rows (`#1..#15` `checks[].links`) accept validated `http://` or `https://` URLs (`sanitizeExternalUrl` / `sanitizeExternalLinksList` block `javascript:`, `data:`, `vbscript:`, and `file:` schemes) and preserve clickable links alongside `Broker` and all dossier fields across the web UI, Watchlist search bar, Activity Log Stream 3 change history, Printable HTML Dossier, CSV Export, Work JSON, and downloadable Word (`.docx`) Dossier.
3. **Single `Mark Checked` Workflow & Muted Completed Rows:**
   - Each checklist row renders a single **`Mark Checked`** button that stamps the reviewer date, transitions `"To Do"` rows to **`"In Progress"`** (leaving `"In Progress"` and `"Flagged"` rows active rather than prematurely marking them `"Complete"`), and applies subtle `.wl-row-done` visual muting strictly when a reviewer sets a row's status dropdown to `"Complete"` or `"N/A"`.
4. **Countywide Dangerous Buildings CSV Cross-Checker (`DONDB*.csv`):**
   - Clicking **`Cross-Check Dangerous Buildings CSV`** uploads a periodic City of Houston Department of Neighborhoods `DONDB*.csv` list wholly in the browser (`parseAndMatchDangerousBuildingsCsv`) and matches rows across designated/watched Tier 1 properties (Active Property Watchlist, City Landmarks, COH Historic Districts, NRHP properties, Good Brick winners, and `curated_overrides.json`), countywide pre-1976 Tier 2 structures (`address_shards`), and `Parcel ID Warnings` (flagging scientific-notation corrupted HCAD IDs such as `1.523E+11` while zero-padding 10-to-12-digit HCAD integers to 13 digits). Flagged historic buildings can be added to the Watchlist (auto-populating Check `#13` with the `DONDB` Case #, Section, and Process State) or copied via **`Copy City Notification Summary`**.
5. **Annual (`2020-2025`) & `2026 YTD` Demolition Permit Benchmarks & Screening Methodology (`#demoBenchmarksDrawer` & `#permit-watch-modal`):**
   - Both `app/plat-monitor.html` (`📊 Demolition Stats (2020-2026)` topbar button and summary stage card) and `app/index.html` (`#permit-watch-modal` -> `Annual & 2026 YTD Demolition Permit Benchmarks`) provide a 3-table grant and annual-report reference panel with a 1-click **`📋 Copy Grant / Annual Report Summary`** button (`formatDemolitionBenchmarksGrantText` in `app/js/platMonitorHelpers.js`).
   - **Official Grant Citations:**
     - **City of Houston (Table 1):** *City of Houston, Houston Public Works, Building Code Enforcement - Sold Permits Database (`houstonpermittingcenter.org`)* (Permit Type `SD` Standard Demolition + Permit Type `4D` Dangerous Building Demolition).
     - **HCAD Countywide (Table 2):** *Harris County Appraisal District (`HCAD`) Real Property CAMA Permit Roll (`Real_acct_owner.zip -> permits.txt`, Permit Types `13`, `33` & `43`).*
     - **HAHC Historic Districts (Table 3):** *City of Houston Planning & Development Department - Houston Archaeological and Historical Commission (`HAHC`) Monthly COA Action Packets (`houstontx.gov/planning/Commissions/HAHC.html`).*
   - **How Pools, Partial/Addition Remodels & Detached Garages Are Screened:**
     - In the City of Houston system, interior non-load-bearing wall demolitions (`DEMO OF INTERIOR NON-LOAD BEARING WALLS`), rear-addition removals for remodels (`RESIDENTIAL DEMO FOR FUTURE REMODEL`), and standard swimming pool fill-ins (`EXISTING POOL DEMO 2021 IBC`) are issued under **Permit Type `13` (`BUILDING PMT`)** rather than Demolition Permits (`SD`/`4D`), so `289-397` remodel/pool building permits per year are already excluded. Within `SD`+`4D`, detached garages/sheds (`118` in 2026 YTD, `151` in 2025, `210` in 2024) and the tiny fraction (`< 0.7%`) of sewer-connected pools (`1` in 2026 YTD, `5` in 2025, `5` in 2024) or commercial canopies (`2` in 2026 YTD, `10` in 2025, `4` in 2024) are broken out separately, yielding **`1,634` Net Full Main-Building Teardowns in 2026 YTD** (out of `1,755` total `SD`+`4D`), **`2,021` in 2025** (out of `2,187`), and **`2,161` in 2024** (out of `2,380`).
     - In HCAD's countywide permit roll, Remodel/Addition permit codes (`Types 11 & 31`, `397-531/yr`) are excluded, and `40-88` pool/spa removals, `19-43` partial/addition demos, and `91-203` detached garage/shed demos per year are screened out of codes `13`/`33`/`43` to yield **Net Countywide Full Main-Building Teardowns** (`2,542` in 2025 out of `2,791`; `2,561` in 2024 out of `2,869`; `2,325` in 2023 out of `2,592`).

## 5. Workspace Mode 3 (`What's New & Daily Log`): Morning Briefing, 90-Day Run Ledger & Honest Sweep Telemetry

To answer *"What happened while I was away, and did the automated daily sweeps actually succeed?"*, `app/plat-monitor.html` includes a third workspace tab (`#modeActivityBtn`, `data-mode="activity"`) backed by a hybrid **CI Snapshot Diff Ledger + Live Browser Delta Synthesizer**:

1. **Daily CI Snapshot Diffing & 90-Day `America/Chicago` Retention (`pipeline/atlas_pipeline/build_monitor_activity_log.py` & `.github/workflows/update_live_permits.yml`):**
   - During every scheduled 7:00 AM Central Time GitHub Actions run (`update_live_permits.yml`), Step 1 runs `build_monitor_activity_log.py --save-before /tmp/atlas_monitor_before` *before* any fetcher runs to snapshot the prior published `property_watchlist.json`, `plat_agenda_monitor.json`, `active_permit_alerts.json`, and `hahc_coa_tracker.json`.

2. **Honest 8-Agency Sweep Health Tracking (`lastSweepHealth` in `fetch_plat_and_watchlist.py`):**
   - Each watched property's daily sweep evaluates all 8 automated feeds (`chk-04` TX Comptroller, `chk-05` HCAD GIS/CAMA, `chk-06` TABC, `chk-09` COH Plat Agendas, `chk-10` TDLR TABS, `chk-13` Houston 311, `chk-14` COH Sold Permits, and `chk-15` TX DSHS Asbestos) and records explicit per-agency outcomes (`ok`, `skipped` when a dossier lacks identifiers for that portal, or `failed` with the diagnostic error text).
