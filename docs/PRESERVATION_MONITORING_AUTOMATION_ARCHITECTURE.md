## 1. Overview of the Two Preservation Monitoring Workflows

Preservation Houston operates two connected early-warning workflows designed with Emily Ardoin:

1. **Workflow A: Biweekly Planning Commission Plat & Variance Review**
   - Screens 100 to 140 subdivision plats, replats, Section I.K Development Plat Variances (`DPV`), and Section IV Public Hearing notices every two weeks ahead of the Thursday 2:30 p.m. Houston Planning Commission meeting across rolling multi-cycle history (`October 29, 2026`, `October 15, 2026`, `October 1, 2026`, and `All Recent Cycles`).
2. **Workflow B: 15-Point Endangered Property Multi-Agency Watchlist & Dossier Workstation**
   - Replaces manual multi-property tracking spreadsheets with a continuous 15-point dossier covering city, county, state, and commercial signals across four stages: `Every Check`, `Sale & Ownership`, `Plans & Use`, and `Demolition Signals`.
   - Uses a unified **Street-Address-First** sidebar convention across both modes (**Street Address** as the bold primary title, **Historic Landmark / Project Name** as the subtitle), sorts Watchlist properties strictly `Tier 1 -> Tier 2 -> Tier 3`, decouples the Watchlist top bar from Planning Commission cycle selectors (showing the `8-Agency Auto-Sweep` timestamp and `Cross-Check Dangerous Buildings CSV` launcher instead, while displaying a `PC MM/DD/YYYY` chip only on plat-originated entries), and supports both **Tracker Status** (`Active / High Priority`, `Dormant / Medium Priority`, `For Sale`, `Monitoring`, `Saved`, `Lost`, `Archived (Tier 3)`) and **Threat Category** (`Significant Building Threatened`, `Neighborhood / District Context Impact`, `Lot Split / Subdividing Historic Lot`, `Setback / Walkable Realm Variance`, `Institutional / Cultural Site`, `No Historic Impact`).

## 2. Workflow A (Biweekly Plat Review): Multi-Cycle Ingestion, Screening & Explicit Watchlist Promotion

| Review Stage | Automation Status | Technical Implementation & Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Multi-Cycle Agenda Ingestion (Excel + PDF Outline + Public Notices)** | **100% Automated** | `fetch_plat_and_watchlist.py` discovers and ingests active and recent Planning Commission cycles (`2026-10-29`, `2026-10-15`, `2026-10-01`) from `houstontx.gov/planning/DevelopRegs/dev_reports.html`, `commiss_plan.html`, and `houstontx.gov/planning/notices/`.<br>**Floored Excel Serial Dates:** Planning Commission Excel spreadsheets encode `PC Date` with fractional meeting-time offsets (for example, `46310.6041666667` for October 15, 2026 at 2:30 p.m.). Both Python (`excel_serial_to_iso`) and JS (`excelSerialToIso`) floor the serial integer (`int(math.floor(float(val)))`) before adding to the `1899-12-30` epoch so `10/15/2026` never rounds up to `10/16/2026`.<br>**Dynamic Hearing Dates, Outline Early Warnings & Notice PDF Separation:** Section III/IV items carry future public hearing dates three to six weeks after the establishment agenda (for example, `November 12, 2026` and `December 3, 2026` on the October 29, 2026 agenda). Even before individual Notice PDFs are posted, `build_plat_agenda_dataset()` synthesizes early-warning Section III/IV items directly from the Agenda Outline PDF (`PH-131..PH-138`). When only a 2-page public hearing notice exists (`Montrose Residences`, `2026-1585`), `pdf` remains `""` and `noticePdf` stores the notice URL alongside `hearingDateIso`, so the UI renders `Public Hearing Notice PDF (Nov 12, 2026)` rather than mislabeling a notice letter as a Plat Plan drawing or misclassifying Chapter 42 notice boilerplate as a `DPV`. |
| **Pass 1: Rule-Based Screening & Recoverable Screen-Out Reasoning** | **100% Automated + 1-Click Override** | Separates City Historic District items (`Designated`), Extraterritorial Jurisdiction (`ETJ`) items, and low-risk administrative plats (first-time plats with no previous subdivision and zero risk/institutional/pre-1976 signals, street dedications `SP`, general plans `GP`, and pure utility sites). Every screened-out card displays its exact Pass 1 reasoning pill (`reason`) and includes an **`Override Screen-Out -> Move to To Check`** button so staff can promote any edge case into the active `To check` queue. |
| **Pass 2: Multi-HCAD Oldest Building Age & Countywide Map Verification** | **~85% Automated / ~15% Assisted** | **Multi-HCAD Oldest Nonzero Structure Rule:** Replats and commercial assemblages frequently span three or four HCAD accounts mixing post-2000 infill or vacant (`year_built == 0`) lots with a c. 1940 structure (`Amerimex Motors`). Both `resolve_multi_parcel_building_tier` (Python) and `resolveMultiParcelBuildingTier` (JS) parse comma/semicolon/whitespace-separated 13-digit HCAD IDs, ignore `year_built == 0` placeholders, check `curated_overrides.json` architectural dates first, and compute `oldestYearBuilt = min(year >= 1836)` across all structures on all associated accounts so a 1940 building is never masked by a 2013 building on an adjacent parcel.<br>**Countywide Parcels & Vector Street Labels on the Atlas Map:** When Column Z (`HCAD Account No.`) is blank, reviewers can switch the main Houston Building Atlas map (`app/index.html`) to `Parcels` or `Both` (`hcadViewportParcels.js` rendering raster parcel outlines from `>= 14.5` and interactive vector parcel fills from `https://arcweb.hcad.org/server/rest/services/public/public_query/MapServer/0/query` at zoom `>= 15.0` / `>= 15.2`, deduplicated against Pilot Core parcels and including vacant `year_built == 0` lots) with crisp vector street name labels (`vector-street-labels` / `transportation_name` from `tiles.openfreemap.org`) to click and lock in all affected 13-digit HCAD accounts. |
| **Pass 3: Explicit Watchlist Confirmation, Two-Way Tier Sync & Cross-Cycle Continuity** | **Human Judgment with Data-Loss Guards** | **Explicit Confirmation (`Confirm Tier & Add to Watchlist`):** Auto-assigned Tier 1 and Tier 2 recommendations on biweekly agenda items remain unconfirmed until a reviewer clicks **`Confirm Tier & Add to Watchlist`** (or confirms/changes the tier).<br>**Two-Way Tier Sync & Soft-Archive Guard:** Changing a previously promoted item to `Tier 1` or `Tier 2` updates the linked Watchlist entry's `tier` immediately. Downgrading a promoted item to `Tier 3 (Low / Note)` checks `hasHumanDossierContributions(entry)`: if zero human notes, result links, documents, or custom search terms exist, the auto-stub is cleanly removed; if human research exists, the dossier is preserved under `trackerStatus = "Archived (Tier 3)"` (`tier = "3"`) so staff research is never lost.<br>**Cross-Cycle Deferral & Resubmission Continuity:** `link_cross_cycle_history` (Python) and `linkCrossCycleHistory` (JS) link deferred or withdrawn and resubmitted applications across cycles by `appNo`, shared 13-digit HCAD accounts, or normalized project subdivision name + geographic/applicant context (`buildContinuityKeys`), displaying `Prior Planning Commission Agenda History` badges (`priorCycles`) and carrying forward prior review notes and Watchlist links without colliding distinct section phases (`Sec 1` vs `Sec 2`). |

## 3. Workflow B (15-Point Property Watchlist): Daily 8-Agency Sweeps, 3-Way Cloud Reconciliation & Assisted Controls

Out of the 15 monitoring points in the Endangered Property Watchlist, **8 points are swept automatically every day at 7:00 AM Central Time (`12:00 UTC`)**, and **7 points provide policy-compliant assisted launchers** tailored to each agency's search rules.

### Part 3A: The 8 Daily Automated Checklist Points & Ownership-Partitioned 3-Way Reconciliation

Every day at 7:00 AM Central Time (`.github/workflows/update_live_permits.yml`), `fetch_plat_and_watchlist.py` pulls live staff Watchlist edits from `Plat_&_Watchlist_Sync`, executes `sweep_watched_property_agencies()` across every watched property (matching all perimeter street addresses, 13-digit HCAD accounts, and discovered snowball `entities`, `dbas`, `officers`, and `projectNumbers`), and stamps `lastAutoSweepAt` and `[AUTO-SWEEP]` findings by stable `row.id` (`chk-01..chk-15`):

1. **Check #4: Corporate Entity Verification (`TX Comptroller Sales & Franchise Tax`)**
   - **Automated Endpoints:** `data.texas.gov` Socrata REST APIs for Active Sales Tax Permit Holders (`https://data.texas.gov/resource/jrea-zgmq.json`) and Active Franchise Taxpayers (`https://data.texas.gov/resource/9cir-efmm.json`), querying Harris County / Houston perimeter street addresses and known LLC/DBA names (`Smith St C Store LLC`, `OB Texas Properties LLC`).
   - **Assisted Fallbacks (when no franchise tax filing exists yet):** Entity/officer/DBA copy pills (never street-address copy pills) paired with 1-click links to Texas Comptroller Taxable Entity Search (`https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/franchise/account-status/search`), Bizapedia (`https://www.bizapedia.com/`), Google Web Search, and TX SOSDirect (`https://direct.sos.state.tx.us/acct/acct-login.asp`).
2. **Check #5: HCAD Ownership & Classification (`HCADGIS` / CAMA)**
   - **Automated Endpoints:** Live HCAD ArcGIS REST MapServer (`https://arcweb.hcad.org/server/rest/services/public/public_query/MapServer/0/query`) and annual HCAD CAMA real property tables, detecting owner-of-record transfers, mailing address changes, valuation shifts, and state land-use reclassifications.
3. **Check #6: Liquor Licensing (`TABC Public Inquiry & Open Data`)**
   - **Automated Endpoints:** `data.texas.gov` Socrata REST APIs for TABC Pending Original License Applications (`https://data.texas.gov/resource/mxm5-tdpj.json`, queried on `address` and `owner` in Harris County because `mxm5-tdpj` omits `trade_name`) and Active Licenses (`https://data.texas.gov/resource/7hf9-qc9f.json`, queried across `address`, `owner`, and `trade_name` with brand-variant expansion such as `Brake Time` / `Braketime`).
4. **Check #9: Replats & Variances (`COH Planning Commission Agenda & Notices`)**
   - **Automated Endpoints:** Biweekly COH Plat Spreadsheets (`https://www.houstontx.gov/planning/DevelopRegs/dev_reports.html`), Agenda Outline PDFs (`Section I.K DPV` & Section III/IV Hearings), and Public Hearing Notice PDFs (`https://www.houstontx.gov/planning/notices/`). Because Microsoft PowerApps PlatTracker restricts login to registered applicants/surveyors with no public search interface, Check #9 links directly to the in-app Biweekly Plat Review, `dev_reports.html`, and Planning Commission Notices instead of the login wall.
5. **Check #10: Accessibility Registration (`TDLR Architectural Barriers - TABS`)**
   - **Automated Endpoint & Canonical UI Link:** Posts `application/x-www-form-urlencoded` DataTables queries (`build_tdlr_tabs_search_params`, omitting empty filter keys so server-side filters bind cleanly) to `https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/TABS/Search/SearchProjects` with `LocationCounty="2101"` (Harris County) and enriches matched projects via `https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/TABS/Search/Project/{ProjectNumber}` for commercial construction or renovation registrations >= \$50,000, while linking reviewers directly to `https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/tabs/search`.
6. **Check #13: Code Complaints & Dangerous Buildings (`Houston 311` + `DONDB*.csv` Cross-Checker)**
   - **Automated Endpoint & Periodic City List Cross-Checker:** Automatically sweeps the City of Houston 311 ArcGIS FeatureServer (`HOUSTON311_RECENT_SR_SNOW/FeatureServer/0`) for open structural/nuisance complaints, and pairs with the in-browser **`Import & Cross-Check Dangerous Buildings CSV`** workstation (`parseAndMatchDangerousBuildingsCsv`) for periodic City of Houston Department of Neighborhoods `DONDB*.csv` exports (`CaseNumber`, `Parcel.ParcelID` with 10-to-12-digit zero-padding to 13 digits and scientific-notation warnings, `Parcel.ParcelName`, `Parcel.Section`, `Parcel.Area.Code`, `ProcessState.Code`, as well as street-split CSV layouts).
7. **Check #14: Demolition, Sewer Disconnect & Building Permits (`COH Sold Permits`)**
   - **Automated Endpoints:** City of Houston Sold Permits portal (`https://cohtora.houstontx.gov/approot/soldpermits/online_permit.htm`) and `active_permit_alerts.json` (`fetch_live_permits.py`). Redundant weekly summary `Permit Activity Reports` links are omitted on per-property dossiers because Sold Permits already contains every individual permit record and automatically harvests 8-digit ILMS Project Numbers into `searchTerms.projectNumbers` for Check #8 (`ILMS`).
8. **Check #15: Asbestos & Demolition Notifications (`TX DSHS`)**
   - **Automated Endpoint:** Texas Department of State Health Services Asbestos Notification search (`https://vo.ras.dshs.state.tx.us/datamart/notificationSearchAction.do`), catching mandatory 10-working-day advance abatement and demolition filings.

**Ownership-Partitioned 3-Way Watchlist Reconciliation (`reconcileWatchlistProperty`):**  
Previously, when `Plat_&_Watchlist_Sync` held a cloud sheet row saved on an earlier date (such as October 23, 2026), loading the cloud sheet could overwrite newer automated pipeline findings or seeded snowball `searchTerms` in `property_watchlist.json`. Both `fetch_plat_and_watchlist.py` and `app/js/watchlistSyncHelpers.js` now enforce **field-level ownership partitioning**:
- **Pipeline-Owned Telemetry** (`lastAutoSweepAt` and `[AUTO-SWEEP]` checklist findings deduplicated by `getAutoSweepDedupeKey(rowId, entry)`) is union-merged by `row.id` (`chk-01..chk-15`) so fresh daily sweeps are never wiped by older Cloud Sheet or `localStorage` rows.
- **Human-Owned Research** (`tier`, `tierConfirmed`, `trackerStatus`, `threatCategory`, `legalDescription`, `neighborhood`, `designFirm` hydrated seamlessly from legacy `architectEngineer`, `phPastActions`, `reportedBy`, `driveLinks` tracked via `driveLinksUpdatedAt`, `searchTerms` tracked via `searchTermsUpdatedAt`, and per-checkpoint manual `status`, `lastChecked`, and `checks[].links`) always preserves staff edits and intentional deletions from Cloud Sync / `localStorage`.

## 4. Workstation Features Supporting Multi-Property Investigations

1. **Snowball Search-Term Discovery Panel (`searchTerms`):**
   - Real-world investigations often chain across agencies: on the 1926 John Henry Kirby Mansion (`2000 Smith St.`), searching `"2000 Smith"` and perimeter `"530 Pierce"` uncovered TABC and TDLR TABS (`TABS2026022839`) filings naming `"Smith St C Store LLC"`, `"Brake Time"` / `"Braketime Smith Road"`, and `"Heights Engineering"`; Bizapedia and Comptroller lookups uncovered `"Omair Bashir"` and `"BTCM National Retail Operations LLC"`; searching Block `"12"` and Subdivision `"Hadley"` on Harris County Clerk `RP.aspx` uncovered two same-day warranty deeds revealing `"OB Texas Properties LLC"`.
   - Adding a discovered term (`entities`, `dbas`, `officers`, `legalKeywords`, `altAddresses`, or `projectNumbers`) to the dossier immediately updates all 1-click portal copy pills on Checkpoints `#1`, `#3`, `#4`, `#6`, `#7`, `#8`, `#10`, `#11`, and `#12` without arbitrary truncation and feeds subsequent daily 8-agency automated sweeps.
2. **Inline Property Metadata Editing & Sanitized Document / Google Drive Links:**
   - Every watched property includes an **`Edit Property Details`** form to fill in or update blanks after creation (`Address`, `Secondary Perimeter Addresses`, `Historic / Property Name`, `13-Digit HCAD Account(s)`, `Year Built`, `Legal Description`, `Priority Tier`, `Tracker Status`, `Threat Category`, `Designation / Historic Significance`, `Neighborhood`, `Current Owner`, `Buyer / Developer / Tenant Entities`, `Design / Engineering Firm`, `PH Past Actions`, and `Reported By`).
   - Both the property header (`driveLinks`) and individual checklist rows (`#1..#15` `checks[].links`) accept validated `http://` or `https://` URLs (`sanitizeExternalUrl` / `sanitizeExternalLinksList` block `javascript:`, `data:`, `vbscript:`, and `file:` schemes) and preserve clickable links with XML-safe escaping (`escapeXmlText`) across the web UI, Printable HTML Dossier, CSV Export, and downloadable Word (`.docx`) Dossier.
3. **Single `Mark Checked` Workflow & Muted Completed Rows:**
   - Each checklist row renders a single **`Mark Checked`** button that stamps the reviewer date, transitions `"To Do"` rows to **`"In Progress"`** (leaving `"In Progress"` and `"Flagged"` rows active rather than prematurely marking them `"Complete"`), and applies subtle `.wl-row-done` visual muting strictly when a reviewer sets a row's status dropdown to `"Complete"` or `"N/A"`.
4. **Countywide Dangerous Buildings CSV Cross-Checker (`DONDB*.csv`):**
   - Clicking **`Cross-Check Dangerous Buildings CSV`** uploads a periodic City of Houston Department of Neighborhoods `DONDB*.csv` list wholly in the browser (`parseAndMatchDangerousBuildingsCsv`) and matches rows across designated/watched Tier 1 properties (Active Property Watchlist, City Landmarks, COH Historic Districts, NRHP properties, Good Brick winners, and `curated_overrides.json`), countywide pre-1976 Tier 2 structures (`address_shards`), and `Parcel ID Warnings` (flagging scientific-notation corrupted HCAD IDs such as `1.523E+11` while zero-padding 10-to-12-digit HCAD integers to 13 digits). Flagged historic buildings can be added to the Watchlist (auto-populating Check `#13` with the `DONDB` Case #, Section, and Process State) or copied via **`Copy City Notification Summary`**.
